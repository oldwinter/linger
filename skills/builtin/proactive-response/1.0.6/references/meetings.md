# Meetings, interviews and appointments

One meeting, interview or appointment is one Tracking Item of kind `meeting`; it stays open until it is over.

## Routines it usually needs

- **Preparation reminder**: about 30 minutes before (longer for something that needs travel), with what to bring or read.
- For an appointment that must be confirmed or booked in advance, one reminder when that should happen.
- **The evening before**, when the next day holds several meetings or people the Human meets for the first time: one look at the whole day.

## When to tell

- The evening before: who the Human meets, when and where, and what each meeting is about or what they want from it. Leave out routine meetings that need nothing.
- Before it starts: time, title or place, people, and what the Human needs to bring. Point out what does not add up between sources (a changed time, a figure that differs between the deck and the mail).
- The time, place or link changed, or it was cancelled: tell at once.

## When things change

- Rescheduled: update the item's `due_at` and move its Routines.
- Cancelled, or its time has passed: mark the item done and delete its Routines. A reminder before the meeting does not finish it.

## Preparing

The special cases below say which meetings are worth preparing and what to look up; their Routine gets a `Look up first:` line. History and promises are in the Human's knowledge, recordings and mail; anything outside the Human's own material comes from the web, and a meeting that needs both names both.

What to make follows Documents and decks in SKILL.md: a few lines in the reminder when little turned up or little is known; a prep document when there is more, as is usual for an investor, client or partner with history to review; a deck when the Human presents to this audience and has none. Set the Routine early enough to finish it; a deck usually needs the day before. When nothing turned up, say so.

Remind and prepare unless the Human declined or the meeting was cancelled: many people never formally accept an invitation, so a missing reply is not a no. Never make an accepted invitation or a confirmed time a condition in the Routine's `--content`. Put the `Look up first:` line, what to make, who will read it and what they need it for into the Routine's `--content` when you arrange it, since the Routine does not read this guide when it fires. Shape the document or deck for that reader and purpose: help the Human prepare for the meeting or appointment, or prepare material they can share with the intended audience. Include only what serves that purpose.

## Special cases

- **An interview, a podcast or press conversation, or a first meeting with someone**: worth preparing. Look up what the Human said before on the topics they will be asked about, and public background on the person or outlet from the web.
- **A meeting with an investor, client or partner**: worth preparing. Look up where things were left last time and what they asked for or are waiting on. When there were earlier meetings with the same people, go through them in order: when each was, what was discussed, what moved forward, what is still open or promised, and what to raise this time.
- **A meeting where the Human presents, pitches, demos or decides**: worth preparing. Look up the state of the materials, and the outside facts the meeting needs, such as the latest on the topic, the market or the other side's recent news. These come from the web; the Human's own mail and knowledge will not have them.
- **A recurring or internal meeting, an all-hands, or a personal appointment** such as a doctor, a haircut or a repair visit: needs only a reminder unless the Human presents, pitches, demos or decides; then prepare as above. Otherwise say the time, the place or link, and what to bring.
