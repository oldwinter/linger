# Work Items and Dashboard

Work Items and Dashboard covers creating tracked issues on the four-column board, inspecting and commenting inside the deep-linked `WorkItemDetailDrawer`, mutating and archiving items in `WorkItemPeek`, reading the full-page narrative story, and handing off work directly from a Room.

## Sub-features

- Viewing active Work Items, filtering (`Filter by title`, `Room`, `Priority`, `Assignment`, `Execution`, `Archived`, `Sort`), side-by-side comparison (`/t/:tenantId/dashboard/side-by-side`), and exporting (`Export & share`) at `/t/:tenantId/dashboard`
- Creating issues from the Dashboard (`New issue` dialog with `Title`, `Description`, `Issue priority`, `Origin Room`)
- Deep-linked `WorkItemDetailDrawer` (`<aside aria-label="Work Item details">`) opened automatically on `/t/:tenantId/dashboard/:workItemId`, supporting metadata inspection, commenting (`Add a comment` + `Comment`), and `Close detail`
- Modal `WorkItemPeek` (`<dialog>`) opened by clicking a board card, supporting inline `Edit`, state transitions, assignment, comments (`Submit`), `Open full page`, and `Archive` / `Unarchive`, plus the read-only full-page story at `/t/:tenantId/work-items/:workItemId` (also reachable via each card's `What happened` link)
- Handing work off directly from a Room (`/t/:tenantId/rooms/:roomId/hand-off`) and jumping to the resulting Dashboard item (`See it on the dashboard`)

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click `Dashboard` in `Primary navigation` to open `/t/:tenantId/dashboard` and use `New issue` to create tracked work.
- Navigating to `/t/:tenantId/dashboard/:workItemId` (after creating an issue, clicking `See it on the dashboard`, or reloading a deep link) renders the board with `WorkItemDetailDrawer` open on the right (`Close detail` returns to `/t/:tenantId/dashboard`).
- Clicking a board card opens `WorkItemPeek` (`<dialog>`) in place; clicking `Open full page` inside `WorkItemPeek` (or `What happened` under a card) navigates to the read-only story page `/t/:tenantId/work-items/:workItemId`.
- From an active non-thread Room, open the Room **header** `More actions for <Room>` menu -> `Hand off` to navigate to `/t/:tenantId/rooms/:roomId/hand-off`, enter what needs doing, and hand it to a target member.

## Driving it with verify-linger.mjs

Preconditions: Stack is running, `doctor` passed, browser session is signed in, and viewport width is at least `1024px` (default `1280x720`) so `WorkItemDetailDrawer` renders beside the board instead of overlaying it.

- **Create issue on Dashboard and comment in `WorkItemDetailDrawer`**: Click `find role link --name "Dashboard" --exact click`, wait for `--fn "window.location.pathname.endsWith('/dashboard')"` and `--text "Dashboard"`, and snapshot (`snapshot -i`). Click the header `button "New issue"` by `@e` ref (an empty board renders two `New issue` buttons), fill textbox `Title` with `Verify dashboard persistence`, optionally fill `Description`, and click button `Create issue`. Wait for `--fn "/\\/t\\/[^/]+\\/dashboard\\/[^/]+$/.test(window.location.pathname)"` and `aside[aria-label="Work Item details"]`, record `<workItemId>` from `get url`, and capture `work-item-detail.aria.txt` and `work-item-detail.png`. Fill `Add a comment` inside `WorkItemDetailDrawer` with `Drawer comment check`, click `find role button --name "Comment" --exact click`, and wait for `--text "Drawer comment check"`.
- **Inspect `WorkItemPeek`, archive/unarchive across reload, and open full-page story**: Snapshot (`snapshot -i`), click the board card link matching `Verify dashboard persistence` (`@e` ref) to open `WorkItemPeek` (`dialog[open]`), verify `Drawer comment check` appears in `Recent comments`, and capture `work-item-peek.aria.txt` and `work-item-peek.png`. Click the exact `@e` ref for button `Archive` inside `WorkItemPeek`, wait for `--text "Unarchive"`, then reload (`browser --run-id "<id>" -- reload`). Reload closes `WorkItemPeek` (`dialog[open]` absent) while keeping `WorkItemDetailDrawer` (`aside[aria-label="Work Item details"]`) open and the deep-linked card visible on `/dashboard/<workItemId>`. Re-snapshot, re-click the board card link, confirm `Unarchive` persisted, click the `@e` ref for `Unarchive`, wait for `--text "Archive"`, then click `find role link --name "Open full page" click` and verify URL transitions to `/t/:tenantId/work-items/<workItemId>` showing `What happened`, `Back to the Dashboard`, and no `Archive`/`Unarchive` buttons (`work-item-story.png`).
- **Hand off from a Room**: Inside a non-thread Room with another participant (for example `Release verification room`, `Linger`'s direct Room, or starter Room `Getting Started`), click `section[aria-label="Room <Room>"] button[aria-label="More actions for <Room>"]` (the Room header menu, not the sidebar row), click menuitem `Hand off`, wait for `--fn "window.location.pathname.endsWith('/hand-off')"`, fill textbox `What needs doing` with `Follow up on room verification`, click button `Hand it to <name>`, wait for `--text "See it on the dashboard"`, capture `hand-off.aria.txt` and `hand-off.png`, and click `find role link --name "See it on the dashboard" click` to confirm it lands on `/t/:tenantId/dashboard/<workItemId>` with `aside[aria-label="Work Item details"]` open.
- **Cross-check TeamHub / PostgreSQL**: Run read-only `api --run-id "<id>" GET "/v0/tenants/<tenantId>/work-items"` (defaults to `archived=false`; add `?archived=true` to include archived items), `api --run-id "<id>" GET "/v0/work-items/<workItemId>"`, `api --run-id "<id>" GET "/v0/work-items/<workItemId>/comments"`, and `sql --run-id "<id>" "SELECT id, number, title, state, priority, archived_at FROM work_items ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 5;"` plus `sql --run-id "<id>" "SELECT work_item_id, body, deleted_at FROM work_item_comments ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 5;"`.

## Gotchas

- An empty Dashboard renders two `button "New issue"` controls (header and empty state). Target the header button by `@e` ref from `snapshot -i`.
- `/t/:tenantId/dashboard/:workItemId` opens `WorkItemDetailDrawer` (`<aside aria-label="Work Item details">`) automatically — only `WorkItemPeek` (`<dialog>`) starts closed and closes on reload. Below `1024px`, `WorkItemDetailDrawer` covers the board full-screen (`inset-0 z-20`), so keep viewport `>= 1024px` or click `Close detail` before clicking board cards. Closing `WorkItemDetailDrawer` navigates back to `/t/:tenantId/dashboard`, hiding an archived item until the `Archived` filter is enabled.
- Never use unscoped `find role button --name "Archive" click` while `WorkItemPeek` is open: it can substring-match the board's `button "Archived"` filter behind the modal. Click `Archive` and `Unarchive` by their exact `@e` refs from `snapshot -i` (or pass `--exact`).
- Both `WorkItemDetailDrawer` (submit button `Comment`) and `WorkItemPeek` (submit button `Submit`) label their comment textarea `Add a comment`; target by `@e` ref when both are mounted.
- Below `640px`, secondary board filters (including `button "Archived"`) collapse behind `button "Filters"`.
- `/t/:tenantId/work-items/:workItemId` (`Open full page` / `What happened`) is a strictly read-only story view without `Archive`/`Unarchive`.
- `More actions for <Room>` appears both in the sidebar list row (no `Hand off`) and in the Room header (`section[aria-label="Room <Room>"]`, which has `Hand off`). Target the header button.
- `Hand off` targets require an eligible non-self Room participant; when verifying from a solo Room, add a member such as `Research Analyst` first, or use `Linger`'s direct Room (`Hand it to Linger`) or starter Room `Getting Started`.
